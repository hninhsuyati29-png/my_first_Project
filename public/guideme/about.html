Why UIT Guide Me?

First-year students, exam candidates, event guests and staff all struggle to find the right room on time. UIT Guide Me removes the confusion with smart, real-time indoor and outdoor navigation.

Turn-by-Turn Directions

Get precise step-by-step routing from any entrance to your destination — across floors, stairs and elevators.

Instant Room Search

Search by room number, lecturer name, faculty or facility and jump straight to it in seconds.

Timetable Integration

Link your class schedule and let the system guide you automatically to your next lecture or lab.

Accessibility Routes

Wheelchair-friendly paths, ramps and elevator-only routes for users with mobility needs.

Real-Time Updates

Room changes, closures and event locations update live so you always follow the correct path.

Works on Any Device

Fully responsive — use it on your phone while walking or on a lobby kiosk. No installation required.

A closer look at how UIT Guide Me works

Interactive indoor maps

Every floor of every building is mapped in detail — classrooms, labs, offices, libraries, restrooms and exits. Tap any room to see its details and get instant directions.

Guiding every member of the campus

Mission, Vision & Goals

We believe finding your way should never stand between you and your education.

Our Mission

To provide every UIT student, lecturer and visitor with a fast, accurate and easy-to-use navigation experience that saves time and eliminates the stress of finding rooms across campus.

Our Vision

To become the smartest, most connected university campus in the region — where technology guides people seamlessly and no one ever feels lost within our walls.

Our Goals

To map 100% of campus facilities, reduce time spent finding rooms by half, support accessibility for all users, and continuously improve using real feedback from our community.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide how we design and improve UIT Guide Me every day.

  • Accuracy — Reliable maps and directions you can always trust.
  • Simplicity — Clean, intuitive design anyone can use instantly.
  • Inclusivity — Accessible navigation designed for everyone.
  • Innovation — Always improving with smart, modern technology.

How UIT Guide Me Works

Reach any room on campus in four easy steps.

  1. Search — Type a room number, faculty, lecturer or facility you want to reach.
  2. Select — Pick your destination from the smart suggestions that appear.
  3. Navigate — Follow the highlighted route with clear turn-by-turn directions.
  4. Arrive — Reach your destination on time — every single time.
